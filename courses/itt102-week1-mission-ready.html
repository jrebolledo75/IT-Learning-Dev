Mission Ready #001 — The Slow Office Computer

Organization
Silver State Medical Clinic
Department
Front Office
Priority
Medium
Business Impact
One employee is losing productivity.

Customer Statement

“My computer has become slower every day over the last month. I have not installed anything new.”

Your Mission

Use the six-step troubleshooting process to identify the most probable cause, recommend a solution, verify functionality, and explain the outcome professionally.

Mission Progress

0% complete

Step 1

Identify the Problem

Gather information, question the user, identify symptoms, and determine what has changed.

Step 2

Establish a Theory of Probable Cause

Question the obvious and consider multiple possible causes before selecting the most likely theory.

Step 3

Test the Theory to Determine the Cause

Choose safe, efficient tests. Record the result of each test and decide whether the theory is confirmed.

Step 4

Establish a Plan of Action and Implement the Solution

Consider business impact, safety, backups, permissions, tools, and whether escalation is required.

Step 5

Verify Full System Functionality and Implement Preventive Measures

Step 6

Document Findings, Actions, and Outcomes
